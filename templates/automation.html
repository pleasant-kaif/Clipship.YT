ACCOUNT ACCESS

YouTube connection

Checking…

Connect your YouTube channel through Google OAuth. Clipship stores its refresh token locally and requests permission only to manage your YouTube account.

  1. In Google Cloud, enable YouTube Data API v3 and create an OAuth client.
  2. Add http://127.0.0.1:5000/api/youtube/callback as a redirect URI.
  3. Upload the downloaded OAuth client JSON. Only explicitly approved clips can upload.

OAuth client and refresh token stay in ignored local files. Videos default to private.

01 / SCOUT

Trending videos

Find trends, then import and publish a local video you own or are authorized to publish.

Country and category filters are applied by YouTube; language and Indian-region matching are explicitly best-effort. Language uses YouTube's relevance-language hint plus local title/description text scoring. India-zone matching only ranks videos whose text mentions a zone's state names; it cannot verify spoken language or creator location, and non-matches may still appear. A fresh search costs 100 quota units plus 1 unit for the separate public statistics/duration lookup when videos are returned (up to 101 total); automatic refreshes reuse matching results for 15 minutes. Like/comment counts may be hidden by a video's owner. Search results are not permission to reuse a video; add a local source only if you own it or have permission.
